The end of August means we must bid adieu to summer. Hello September and hello fall! Get ready to enjoy Oktoberfest season, autumn celebrations, some of the last outdoor dance parties, movies, and markets of the season, as well as cultural festivals, concerts, and more. Soak up the sun and get ready to see some pops of color in the city.


by Yelena Nicolle Salvador


1) Allston Christmas

When: Tue. 9/1

Where: Allston

Cost: Free

Info: The college kids are back, and the city is back to booming with people from all over. Keep your eye out on the sidewalks for free hidden treasures: furniture, home decor, appliances, and more – as everyone settles back into the city.


2) Boston Conversations @ Aeronaut

When: Tue. 9/1

Where: Allston

Cost: Free

Info: Meet new friends by heading to Aeronaut for the monthly Boston Conversations event. You’ll get four rounds of speed-friending with prompts that allow for deep discussions rather than surface-level small talk. 


3) Allston Christmas at the Silhouette Lounge

When: Tue. 9/1

Where: Allston

Cost: $7+

Info: Stop by the local dive, the Sil, for some holiday cheer. Celebrate Allston Christmas with a wintry alcoholic beverage like a $7 peppermint shot or a $10 cocktail. When you buy a special bev, you can also enjoy a $1 glizzie hot dog.


4) Boston Public Garden Swan Boats

When: Tue. 9/1 - Mon. 9/7

Where: Allston

Cost: $4.75

Info: Hop on the swan boats before they retire for the season. These iconic pontoons are a city staple, and they serve as the perfect way to experience the heart of Boston. Soak up the views in the Public Garden, wave to neighbors, and soak up the sun.


5) Thursdays on the Lawn at the Loring Greenough House

When: Thursdays, starting Thu. 9/3

Where: Jamaica Plain

Cost: Free

Info: Watch the sunset in the heart of Jamaica Plain on the lawn of the historic Loring Greenough House. Grab a bite from a local food truck while sipping on a glass of vino with friends and family. There will be pop-up artists, live music, lawn games, and more.


6) Harvest Festival

When: Thu. 9/3 - Mon. 9/7

Where: Downtown

Cost: Free

Info: Stop by Marzae Cellar + Provisions for a Labor Day weekend harvest festival. Sip on free samples of mulled cider and try your hand at stomping grapes with your feet on Saturday. Special food, like cider donuts, and themed drinks will be available for purchase too.
